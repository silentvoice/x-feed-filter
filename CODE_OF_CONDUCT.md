# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We, the contributors and maintainers, make this project a place without harassment for all persons. This includes all ages, body sizes, disabilities, ethnicities, gender identities and expressions, levels of experience, education levels, nationalities, personal appearances, races, religions, and sexual identities and orientations.

## Our standard

This project uses the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). All contributors must obey it in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and other communication about the project.

Examples of correct behavior:

- Be kind and respectful to other persons.
- Accept different opinions and experiences.
- Give and accept useful feedback.
- Accept responsibility for your mistakes, and learn from them.

Examples of incorrect behavior:

- Sexual language or images, and unwanted sexual attention
- Insults, personal attacks, and trolling
- Public or private harassment
- Publication of the private information of other persons without their permission

## Report a problem

1. Use the **Report content** function of GitHub on the comment, issue, or pull request.
2. You can also contact the maintainer, [@silentvoice](https://github.com/silentvoice), through the contact information on the GitHub profile.

The maintainers examine all reports quickly and fairly. They keep the identity of the reporter private.

## Enforcement

The maintainers can remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not obey this code of conduct. They can ban a contributor for a time or permanently. The enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant 2.1 apply.
